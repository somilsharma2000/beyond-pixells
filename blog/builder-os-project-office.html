From spreadsheets to a system: the builder's project office

By Beyond Pixells · 2026-09-26 · 5 min read

A builder's office runs on relationships and spreadsheets — brokers who bring buyers, buyers who pay in slabs, projects that move through milestones. The spreadsheet holds it until the moment it matters: when someone asks a question it cannot answer.

Questions a spreadsheet cannot answer in time

  • Which units are unsold, held or booked — right now, not last Friday?
  • Which buyer's milestone payment is due this week, and whose follow-up is late?
  • Which broker brought which buyer, and what is owed to whom?
  • Which project milestone is behind, and what does that mean for collections?

Builder OS: the project office as a system

Builder OS by Beyond Pixells turns the office into one running system:

  • Project milestones tracked per tower and phase, with status visible to the owner at a glance.
  • Inventory — every unit, its status, its price and its buyer, in one ledger instead of four files.
  • Buyer follow-ups with WhatsApp reminders, so a "will pay next week" becomes a dated next step, not a hope.
  • Milestone-linked collections — payment schedule slabs tied to construction progress, with dues surfaced automatically.
  • Broker management — source of every lead, payouts pending, all attributable.

In real estate, the money follows the milestone. When the milestone slips quietly, the money slips loudly, later.

Built for builder offices, not enterprises

Builder OS is honest about scope: it is a data and operations system for the promoter's office — it does not do legal, tax or RERA filings, which stay with your chartered accountant and counsel. What it does do is make sure the office that feeds those professionals is never guessing.

Frequently asked questions

Can it track brokers and payouts?

Yes — every lead has a source, broker payouts are attributable and pending payouts are visible.
