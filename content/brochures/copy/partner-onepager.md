## Partner FAQ

**1. When and how do I get paid?**  
Upfront setup commissions are transferred via UPI/NEFT within 7 business days of client payment verification. Recurring monthly commissions are aggregated and paid on the 10th of every calendar month.

**2. What happens if my referred client churns or pauses their subscription?**  
Recurring monthly commissions are tied to active paid subscriptions. If a gym pauses or cancels, commission pauses. If the gym re-activates within 90 days, your recurring payout automatically resumes.

**3. Do I need to handle post-sale customer support or tech troubleshooting?**  
No. Beyond Pixells provides direct 1-on-1 WhatsApp and phone support to the gym owner and front-desk staff. You have zero support overhead.

**4. Is this partner agreement exclusive?**  
No exclusivity is required. You can freely recommend Gym OS alongside your existing equipment catalog and setup consultancy without restricting your other vendor relationships.
