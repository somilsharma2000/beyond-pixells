Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: August 28, 2026 • Last Updated: August 28, 2026

1. Overview

At Beyond Pixells, we are dedicated to delivering high-quality gym management software through Gym OS. This Refund & Cancellation Policy details the terms governing setup fees, subscription cancellations, refunds, and data retention.

2. Setup Fee Policy

The setup fee is a one-time charge covering customized website creation, system configuration, staff account setup, and onboarding. Once setup work has commenced, the setup fee is non-refundable.

3. Monthly Subscriptions

Monthly subscriptions are billed in advance on a recurring monthly schedule:

  • If a gym owner cancels mid-month, no prorated refund will be provided for the remaining days of that current billing cycle.
  • Your subscription and access will remain fully active until the end of the paid monthly period.

4. Annual Subscriptions

For clients enrolled in annual subscription plans:

  • Cancellation within 3 Months: If cancelled within the first 3 months of an annual subscription, a 50% refund of the remaining unused months will be issued.
  • Cancellation after 3 Months: If cancelled after 3 months, no monetary refund is provided, but your subscription and platform access will remain active for the full paid annual period.

5. No Lock-in & Easy Cancellation

Gym OS believes in earning your business every month:

  • There are no lock-in contracts, exit fees, or cancellation penalties.
  • Clients can cancel their subscription at any time by providing 7 days written notice prior to their next billing date.

6. Setup Phase Cancellation

If you request cancellation during the initial onboarding phase:

  • Before Work Begins: If cancellation is requested before any design or configuration work has started, a 50% refund of the setup fee will be granted.
  • After Work Begins: If configuration or website creation work has already started, the setup fee is non-refundable.

7. Data Export Grace Period

Upon subscription cancellation, gym owners are provided a 30-day grace period to export all gym data, including member records, lead lists, attendance history, and payment logs. After 30 days, all data associated with the account is permanently deleted from Beyond Pixells servers.

8. Dispute Resolution

If you experience any billing discrepancies or concerns, please contact us first at beyondpixells@gmail.com. We endeavor to resolve all billing inquiries within 3 business days. If a dispute remains unresolved, it may be escalated to the appropriate consumer forum in India.
