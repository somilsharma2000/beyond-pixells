Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 28, 2026 • Last Updated: August 28, 2026

1. Who We Are

Beyond Pixells is the creator and provider of Gym OS, a complete digital operating system and SaaS platform for gym management. We are committed to protecting the privacy of gym owners, staff members, gym members, and prospective leads who interact with our platform.

2. Data We Collect

To deliver our services effectively, Beyond Pixells collects and processes the following categories of information:

  • Gym Owner Data: Name, email address, phone number, gym name, and business address.
  • Gym Member Data: Name, phone number, email address, photo (for digital QR passes), attendance records, membership status, and payment records.
  • Lead Data: Name, phone number, email address, lead source, interest details, and status.
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device information, access times, and system diagnostic logs.

3. How We Store and Protect Your Data

Data security is integral to our platform. All data processed by Gym OS is stored on secure cloud servers using Base44 infrastructure.

  • Encryption in Transit: All web traffic and platform communications are encrypted in transit using HTTPS and modern SSL/TLS protocols.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Strict role-based permission systems ensure data access is restricted to authorized roles only.
  • Infrastructure Security: Servers are protected behind firewalls with active security monitoring.

4. Who Has Access to Data

We enforce strict data access boundaries:

  • Gym Owners & Authorized Staff: Gym owners and their authorized staff (trainers, front desk personnel) have access to their gym's records via role-based permissions granted by the gym owner.
  • Beyond Pixells Technical Staff: Authorized Beyond Pixells technical staff access systems strictly on a need-to-know basis solely for maintenance, support, security audits, or system operation.
  • No Data Selling: Beyond Pixells does not sell or rent personal data to third parties.

5. Data Retention Policy

We retain data only for as long as necessary to fulfill service commitments:

  • Member Data: Member data is retained while the gym's subscription is active. Upon subscription termination, member data is permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • Lead Data: Lead data is retained for up to 12 months to enable lead tracking and conversion analytics.

6. Member & User Rights

Gym members and users have the following rights regarding their personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of their personal data stored in the system.
  • Correction: Request rectification of inaccurate or incomplete personal records.
  • Deletion: Request erasure of personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Data Export: Request a portable export of their personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at beyondpixells@gmail.com.

7. WhatsApp Communications

Gym OS integrates WhatsApp for notifications, digital QR passes, and automated reminders:

  • Opt-In: Members must explicitly opt-in to receive WhatsApp messages.
  • Opt-Out: Members can opt-out at any time by notifying the gym or replying STOP.
  • API Compliance: All messaging strictly complies with WhatsApp Business API policies.

8. Third-Party Services

Gym OS utilizes trusted third-party cloud infrastructure services:

  • Base44: Cloud hosting, database management, and execution infrastructure.
  • QR Code Generation: Dynamic QR code generation via api.qrserver.com.
  • WhatsApp Business API: Messaging and notification delivery platform.

9. Regulatory Compliance (DPDP Act 2023)

Beyond Pixells complies with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023. We handle data lawfully and transparently, acting as a data processor for gym member and lead records.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect software improvements or legal updates. The "Last Updated" date at the top of the page indicates when updates were made.
