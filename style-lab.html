ONE DESIGN

The official visual language of the Beyond Pixells ecosystem. Calm navy canvas, one electric-blue accent, honest light. Applies to every flagship: hub, Gym OS, Dentist OS, Builder OS, blog, email and social.

Operating systems for local businesses.

Logo tile
bp mark: royal blue #1D4BC4 badge, white "bp", pixel-checker accent. Never violet, never gold.

Motion law

Effects must be visible at rest — never hover-only. Hover adds a clean lift (-2px) and a softened shadow, nothing else. No cursor-follow spotlight glow anywhere. 3D tilt, if used, stays at 3°. Scroll reveals are subtle opacity/translate, respecting prefers-reduced-motion.

Grain: .ov-grain fixed overlay at ~50% opacity · Reveals: 400ms ease-out · Reduced motion: all reveals disabled

History

v7.2 "One Design" supersedes: v4 Chrome Violet (violet+gold chrome), v5 Obsidian Veins, v6 gold rims, and all per-product legacy palettes. Hub blue #1D4BC4 = studio; Gym OS navy #0A0F1E = product. Client gym sites intentionally keep their own local branding.
