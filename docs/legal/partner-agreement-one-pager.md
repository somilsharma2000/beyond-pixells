### 1. REFERRAL DEFINITION & QUALIFICATION

1.1. **Qualified Lead**: A prospective gym, fitness studio, or wellness club ("Referred Client") introduced by Partner to Company who:
   - Is not an existing client or active lead in Company's CRM database at the time of submission.
   - Is submitted via Company's official partner portal or registered email within the **Attribution Window**.
   - Executes a binding Master Services Agreement (MSA) / SaaS Subscription Agreement and Data Processing Agreement (DPA) with Company.
   - Completes payment of the required one-time setup fee and first month's subscription.

---

### 2. COMMISSION STRUCTURE & WORKED CALCULATION

2.1. **Founder-Confirmed Commission Structure**:
   - **One-Time Setup Fee Commission**: **30%** of the net collected setup fee.
   - **Recurring Subscription Commission**: **20%** of the net collected monthly SaaS subscription fee for a period of **twelve (12) consecutive months** per Referred Client.

2.2. **Worked Calculation Example** *(Standard Setup: ₹20,000 | Standard Monthly Plan: ₹4,000/month)*:

| Revenue Component | Client Bill Amount | Commission % | Partner Earnings Breakdown |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **One-Time Setup Fee** | ₹20,000 (net of GST) | **30%** | **₹6,000** (payable upon setup fee collection) |
| **Monthly Subscription** | ₹4,000/month (net of GST) | **20%** | **₹800/month** (for 12 months) |
| **12-Month Monthly Sub Total** | ₹48,000 (net of GST) | **20%** | **₹9,600** (cumulative over Year 1) |
| **TOTAL YEAR 1 EARNINGS PER GYM**| **₹68,000 total revenue** | **Blend (~23%)** | **₹15,600 Total Earnings per Gym** |

*Note: All values exclude 18% GST. Commissions are calculated strictly on collected net revenue after deducting payment gateway transaction fees and refunds (`needs CA/legal confirmation`).*

---

### 3. PAYOUT TERMS & TAXATION

3.1. **Payout Schedule**: Commissions are disbursed **monthly**, on or before the **15th day** of each calendar month for net revenues collected by Company during the preceding calendar month.

3.2. **Invoice & Tax Compliance**:
   - If Partner is GST-registered, Partner shall submit a valid Tax Invoice adding 18% GST to commission earnings.
   - Payouts are subject to statutory Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) under **Section 194H of the Income Tax Act, 1961** (Commission/Brokerage) at the applicable statutory rate (2% or 5%) (`needs CA/legal confirmation`).

---

### 4. ATTRIBUTION WINDOW & CONVERSION RULES

4.1. **Attribution Window**: **90 days** from the date Partner formally registers the lead with Company. If a lead converts after 90 days without prior written extension, commission eligibility expires unless re-registered (`needs founder confirmation`).

---

### 5. TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1. **Term**: Valid for 12 months from Effective Date, auto-renewing annually unless terminated.

5.2. **Termination**: Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving **30 days written notice**. Upon termination for convenience, Partner remains entitled to receive tail recurring commissions (20% monthly rate) for the remainder of the 12-month window for all Referred Clients onboarded prior to the termination date, provided those clients continue paying. Immediate termination applies for fraud, illegal acts, or breach of confidentiality.

---

### 6. DPDP ACT & PRIVACY SAFEGUARDS

6.1. **No Unsanctioned Personal Data Transfer**: Partner shall NOT collect, store, process, or transmit personal data of gym members or gym prospects to Company without explicit consent. Partner acts solely as an independent marketing referral agent.

6.2. **Lead Data Safeguards**: Lead introductions shall contain only business contact info (Gym Name, Owner Name, Business Mobile, City) obtained with consent under DPDP Act 2023.
