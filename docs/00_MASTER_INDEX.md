# 0. NON-NEGOTIABLE AGENT MISSION

The agent must treat this document as a RESEARCH AND EXECUTION INDEX, not as a feature checklist.

For EVERY topic and EVERY subtopic:

1. Discover the current state of the market.
2. Search primary sources first.
3. Search strong secondary sources second.
4. Study competitors and adjacent categories.
5. Study customer problems and complaints.
6. Identify established best practices.
7. Identify emerging 2026+ practices.
8. Identify future/next-generation approaches.
9. Identify failure modes.
10. Identify security/privacy/legal implications.
11. Identify automation opportunities.
12. Identify AI-agent opportunities.
13. Identify human approval requirements.
14. Compare implementation approaches.
15. Compare cost, complexity, reliability and scalability.
16. Select an approach only after evidence.
17. Record alternatives considered.
18. Record assumptions.
19. Record unresolved questions.
20. Define measurable success criteria.
21. Implement only after research is sufficient.
22. Test the implementation.
23. Verify the production result.
24. Document what was learned.
25. Recheck the topic before marking it complete.

## DOUBLE-RECHECK RULE

Nothing may be marked COMPLETE after one pass.

Pass A:
- research
- design
- implementation
- test

Pass B:
- independent re-check
- current-market re-check
- edge-case review
- security/privacy review
- UX review
- operational review
- scalability review
- evidence/source review

If a better approach is discovered, update the implementation and documentation.

# CURRENT MARKET PRINCIPLES TO KEEP IN VIEW

The 2026 market is moving beyond conventional feature-heavy SaaS toward software that executes outcomes through agents and workflows. Gartner reports that agentic AI could expose a large portion of traditional enterprise application spend and argues that buyers increasingly care about outcomes and retained institutional/customer context rather than simply adding dashboards or AI features. [Source: Gartner, July 2026]

Enterprise AI coding agents are also expanding across the software-development lifecycle, including planning, implementation, review, testing and orchestration. Gartner describes a move from simple code assistance toward agent-driven development workflows. [Source: Gartner, May/September 2026]

This creates an important strategic implication for Beyond Pixells:

DO NOT BUILD:
"another dashboard with an AI chatbot."

BUILD:
"an operating system that understands the business, remembers its context, monitors its state, executes workflows, asks humans for approval when necessary, and proves the resulting business outcome."

At the same time, agent adoption creates governance and economics problems. Current industry research highlights the need for visibility, control, validation, and sustainable ROI rather than autonomous AI for its own sake.

For SaaS economics, use stage- and segment-specific benchmarks rather than universal targets. Current 2026 benchmark research reports meaningful variation by ARR, business model and customer segment. SaaS Capital, for example, reports 2026 median NRR of 103% and GRR of 91% for bootstrapped B2B SaaS companies in the $3M-$20M ARR range.
