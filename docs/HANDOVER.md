## PART 1 — THE VISION (from the founder, in the words he wants remembered)

### What we are building

**Beyond Pixells is not a web agency. It is a product studio that builds
operating systems for local businesses.**

The founder, **Somil Sharma** (India, WhatsApp: +91 77370 77479), is building a
family of vertical software products — **Gym OS, Dentist OS, Builder OS** — each
one a complete, self-running management system for one industry, plus ventures
(Bloomwire, FORTREX) under the same studio brand. The brand promise, locked by
research: *"We build operating systems for local businesses."*

### The standard: what every single thing must feel like

The founder's bar, expressed in the words he uses — learn these as law:

1. **It must attract.** Everything we ship is a hook — a landing page, a stat,
   a reel, a demo. If it doesn't pull a stranger closer, it isn't finished.
2. **Out of the box.** Nothing ordinary. When a visitor lands anywhere in our
   estate, their reaction must be *"wait, this is different."* Unexpected layouts,
   live-feel UI, motion that feels alive.
3. **Futuristic.** Every product should feel like it arrived from two years
   ahead — the "OS" concept, command centers, live telemetry, AI insights,
   dark premium design. We look and sound like the future of a local business.
4. **Automatic.** The systems we build must run themselves: WhatsApp does the
   follow-ups, reminders do the collecting, dashboards do the reporting, and
   agents (AI) do the monitoring. Automation is not a feature — it is the
   architecture.
5. **Fully working, zero errors.** Nothing ships half-broken. Every form
   tested, every link checked, every failure has a fallback. A lead is never
   lost — even offline, it queues locally and syncs later.
6. **Admin-controllable.** The founder (or any operator) must be able to
   control everything from a dashboard: prices, content, leads, clients. No
   change should require a developer's prayer.
7. **Always more.** The founder wants the system to keep growing — new
   products, new hooks, new automations. Improvements are a permanent feature,
   not a phase.

### The end state — the real goal

> **Beyond Pixells must eventually run without the founder's daily presence —
> a normal, non-technical person should be able to operate it and earn from it.**

That means every process gets documented, automated, and simplified until the
business is a machine: leads flow in on their own, content generates itself
from the design system, sites monitor and heal themselves, and the only human
job left is talking to customers and counting revenue.

**The business model (locked):** services cashflow → products scale. Client
work pays the bills today; the OS family becomes recurring revenue tomorrow.
Pricing architecture: one-time setup + flat monthly INR, unlimited members,
no per-member fees, UPI + GST native.
