The Product

What We Actually Build

This is not a landing page builder. This is a full operating system that runs the business daily.

Public Website

A professional, mobile-responsive website with class schedules, pricing, trainer profiles, and a lead capture form. Every visitor becomes a tracked lead in the CRM automatically.

View Gym OS site →

Management Dashboard

Members, check-ins, payments, leads (kanban), classes, trainers, expenses, and analytics — all in one navy-themed dashboard. The gym owner logs in and runs everything from here.

See live demo →

WhatsApp Automation

Automated renewal reminders, birthday wishes, welcome messages, and class notifications. Members get a QR code for check-in. The system runs on autopilot while the owner sleeps.

QR Check-In System

Members scan a QR code at the gym entrance. The system logs attendance, tracks duration, updates attendance counts, and shows who is in the gym right now — all automatically.

Payment Tracking

Every payment is logged with invoice numbers, GST amounts, methods, and status. The dashboard shows total revenue, pending dues, and monthly breakdowns. No more chasing members manually.

Lead To Member System

A lead fills the website form, appears in the dashboard kanban, gets called, converts to a member, gets a QR code, starts checking in, pays fees — all tracked in one connected system.
