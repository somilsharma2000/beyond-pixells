48 hours from the time you choose your plan. We build your website, import your existing members, generate QR codes, set up WhatsApp automation, and configure everything. You don't lift a finger during setup.

Yes. Book a free demo and we'll show you Gym OS running on a real gym — real members, real check-ins, real payments, real leads. You'll see exactly how it works before you pay anything.

Hosting, maintenance, updates, WhatsApp automation credits, and ongoing support. No surprise charges. If something breaks, we fix it. If you need a new feature, we build it. The monthly fee covers everything.

No lock-in contracts. You pay month-to-month. If you decide Gym OS isn't for you, you simply stop paying and we'll export your member data for you. No penalties, no exit fees.

No. Gym OS runs on any phone, tablet, or laptop. For QR check-in, you just need a phone or tablet at the front desk. We generate the QR codes — you print them on paper or cards. No expensive hardware required.
